Author Guidelines & Policies

Everything you need to prepare, format, and publish your manuscript ethically in IJMRST.

1. Manuscript Preparation & Formatting

Authors must submit original, unpublished manuscripts written in clear English (or verified regional scholarly abstracts where applicable) in .doc / .docx format.

  • Layout & Font: A4 page size, Double-Column format, Times New Roman font (Title: 16pt Bold, Headings: 11pt Bold, Body Text: 10pt Regular).
  • Author Limit: Up to 5 Authors per manuscript (e.g., 4 student researchers + 1 faculty guide) are permitted under the standard publication tier.
  • Structure: Title, Author Details (Name, Designation, Department, Institution, Email), Abstract (150–250 words), 4–6 Keywords, Introduction, Methodology, Results & Discussion, Conclusion, and References (IEEE / APA format).
  • Page Limit: Standard papers should be between 4 to 12 pages, including high-resolution figures and tables.

2. Plagiarism & Publication Ethics (COPE Aligned)

IJMRST enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against plagiarism, data fabrication, and duplicate submissions.

  • Every submitted manuscript undergoes automated similarity screening before peer review.
  • Maximum Acceptable Similarity: Below 15% (excluding bibliography and standard quotes). Papers exceeding 15% similarity are returned for revision or rejected.
  • Double-Blind Peer Review: Author identities are concealed from reviewers and vice versa to ensure unbiased academic evaluation.

3. Open Access & Copyright Policy

IJMRST is a 100% Open Access journal. All published articles are freely accessible worldwide without subscription barriers.

  • Published articles are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).
  • Authors retain full copyright of their work while granting IJMRST the right of first publication and global archival distribution.
